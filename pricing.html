Coffee Catering Packages

Every event is unique, but here's how we typically structure our mobile coffee bar.
Final pricing depends on guest count, menu, and travel.

Petit Ivoire

Small Gatherings (up to ~30 guests)

What's Included:

  • 2 hours of service
  • 1 barista
  • Espresso + one signature latte (Doussou Banani or Gâteau Doumini)
  • Non-caff option (Lait du Dodo)
  • Professional mobile espresso setup
  • Compostable cups, napkins
  • Setup + breakdown

Ideal for: intimate showers, small office events, garage tastings

Sahel Social

Medium Events (~30–75 guests)

What's Included:

  • 2–3 hours of service
  • 2 baristas
  • Espresso menu + 2–3 signature drinks
  • Choice of 1 matcha option (Matcha Mandé or Garden Party)
  • Non-caff option (Lait du Dodo)
  • Choice of 1 cold foam add-on
  • Professional mobile espresso setup
  • Compostable cups, napkins
  • Syrups + dairy / non-dairy milk options
  • Basic signage
  • Setup + breakdown

Optional add-ons: Mama's Beignets, Sahelian Scones

Ideal for: bridal showers, engagement parties, corporate offsites, community events

Café Ivoire Experience

Large Events (75+ guests)

What's Included:

  • 3+ hours of service
  • 2–3 baristas
  • Full menu selection curated for your event
  • All signature drinks: Doussou Banani, Gâteau Doumini, Matcha Mandé, Garden Party
  • Both cold foam options (Caramel Salé + Brown Butter)
  • Non-caff options
  • Professional mobile espresso setup
  • Compostable cups, napkins
  • Syrups + dairy / non-dairy milk options
  • Custom signage
  • Setup + breakdown

Optional add-ons: custom drink naming, Custom Event Branding, beignet bar, Sahelian Scones

Ideal for: weddings, walimas, large corporate events, brand activations

What's Always Included

Professional mobile espresso setup with vintage Nuova Simonelli machine

Full setup + breakdown (you don't lift a finger)

Compostable cups, napkins, stirrers

House-made syrups + dairy / non-dairy milk options

Basic signage with Café Ivoire branding
