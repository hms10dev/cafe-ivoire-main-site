Pricing

One simple rate for private events and brand activations.
Pick your setup, add what you love, and we handle the rest.

Our Rate

$450 base + $12 per guest

Minimum 25 guests

The base covers

  • Full setup and breakdown
  • 3 hours of service
  • Your choice of espresso cart or tap cart

Each guest gets

  • One crafted drink per person
  • From up to 3 signature drinks you choose

Example: 50 guests, 3 hours → $450 + $600 = $1,050, plus 18% service charge = $1,239

An 18% service charge is added to all bookings. Additional service time is $125/hour. Travel fees may apply outside Metro Atlanta.

Choose Your Setup

Same rate either way. The difference is what's poured and how fast.

Espresso Cart

Made to order on our vintage Nuova Simonelli

  • Full espresso menu: Doussou Banani, lattes, americanos
  • Matcha, including Matcha Mandé
  • Each drink built fresh, about 3 minutes per cup
  • Additional barista recommended every ~50 guests ($150)

Best for: weddings, walimas, showers, intimate gatherings where the ritual is part of the experience

Tap Cart

Poured from kegs, about 15 seconds a cup

  • Doucement Cold Brew
  • Cold Brew Latte
  • Bissap Spritz (caffeine-free)
  • No espresso on this setup

Best for: brand activations, launches, and large crowds that need to keep moving

How Booking Works

1. Request a quote. Share your date, guest count, and vision.

2. Secure your date. A signed agreement and non-refundable deposit hold it.

3. Confirm your count. Final guest count is due 7 days before your event.

We take a limited number of private events each month, on purpose. Book early if your date matters.
