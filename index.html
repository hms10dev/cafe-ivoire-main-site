Signature Sahelian Drinks

Doussou Banani

Our plantain latte — house plantain syrup, smooth espresso, warm nostalgia. The one people ask about.

Matcha Mandé

Matcha meets Mandé flavors — subtle nuttiness with a hint of tropical plantain sweetness.

La Minimaliste

Pure matcha, lightly sweetened. Clean and elegant. Nothing extra.

Doucement Cold Brew

Slow-steeped overnight, poured over ice. Smooth, unhurried, exactly what the name says.

Bissap Spritz

Deep red hibiscus, kissed with citrus and sparkle. Vibrant, refreshing, caffeine-free.

Mama T's Beignets

Comfy bites of heaven dusted with sucre. Soft, warm, cloudlike — just like our aunties made.

Every drink carries a memory from home — crafted with plantains, vanilla, butter, strawberry purée, and Sahelian warmth.

Where the Sahel Meets the South
Café Ivoire is a love letter to the Sahel and the South. To plantains roasting in our mothers' kitchens. To attaya poured slowly in small glasses. To long conversations that stretch from Abidjan to Atlanta.
We built this coffee company to make those memories portable — a mobile third space with espresso, matcha, beignets, and a lot of stubborn auntie love.
What We're Building

From cart to café, we're growing slowly, intentionally. Right now that means private events, brand activations, and finding the right residency to grow into.

Diaspora-Driven

Every drink is rooted in West African heritage — never borrowed, always remembered.

Community-Centered

Built to Last
