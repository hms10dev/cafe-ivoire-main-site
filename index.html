Signature Sahelian Drinks

Doussou Banani

Our plantain latte-roasted plantain syrup, smooth espresso, warm nostalgia.

Gâteau Doumini

A dessert-in-a-cup: vanilla, caramelized sugar, espresso layered like birthday cake.

Doucement Cold Brew

Slow-steeped overnight, poured over ice. Smooth, unhurried, exactly what the name says.

Matcha Mandé

Matcha meets Mandé flavors: subtle nuttiness and tropical plantain sweetness.

Garden Party

Earl Grey–infused matcha with fresh strawberry purée.

Mama's Beignets

Hand-cut, cloudlike beignets dusted with powdered sugar. Just like our tanties made.

Every drink carries a memory from home: crafted with plantains, vanilla, butter, strawberry purée, and Sahelian warmth.

Where the Sahel Meets the South

Café Ivoire is a love letter to the Sahel and the South.
To plantains roasting in our mothers' kitchens.
To attaya poured slowly in small glasses.
To long conversations that stretch from Abidjan to Atlanta.

We built this coffee company to make those memories portable—a mobile third space with espresso, matcha, beignets, and a lot of stubborn auntie love.

What We're Building

From cart to café, we're growing slowly, intentionally. Right now that means private events, brand activations, and finding the right residency to grow into.

Diaspora-Driven

Every drink is rooted in West African heritage—never borrowed, always remembered.

Community-Centered

A platform for Black and African artisans, makers, and storytellers to thrive.

Built to Last

Growing with intention, not speed. From cart to café, one step at a time.
