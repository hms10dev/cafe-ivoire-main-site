Where the Sahel Meets the South

Café Ivoire is a love letter to the Sahel and the South.
To plantains roasting in our mothers' kitchens.
To attaya poured slowly in small glasses.
To long conversations that stretch from Abidjan to Atlanta.

We built this coffee company to make those memories portable—a mobile third space with espresso, matcha, beignets, and a lot of stubborn auntie love.

What We're Building

From cart to café, we're growing slowly, intentionally. And right now that means pouring at the events where community already gathers.

Diaspora-Driven

Every drink is rooted in West African heritage—never borrowed, always remembered.

Community-Centered

A platform for Black and African artisans, makers, and storytellers to thrive.

Built to Last

Growing with intention, not speed. From cart to café, one step at a time.
