Our Story

Café Ivoire is a love letter to home—both the one we carry and the one we're building. A space where West African warmth meets Southern hospitality, where every cup holds a memory, and every flavor tells a story.

Born from the rhythms of Abidjan and rooted in the heart of Atlanta, we're here to reimagine what belonging tastes like. This isn't just coffee. It's café—conversation, connection, and culture poured into every sip.
