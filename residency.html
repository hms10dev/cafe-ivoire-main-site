What a Residency Looks Like

A Café Ivoire corner inside your space, on a schedule that works for both of us.

What we bring

  • A licensed, health-compliant mobile coffee bar
  • Espresso or cold brew on tap, matcha, bissap, and Mama T's beignets
  • Our own staff, setup, and breakdown
  • A community that already follows the cart
  • An alcohol-free, family-friendly service environment

What we're looking for

  • A recurring slot: weekly, weekends, or a set season
  • A small footprint with access to power
  • A space in Metro Atlanta with steady foot traffic
  • A partner who cares about the people who walk in

Spaces We'd Love

Bookstores and galleries. People already slow down there. A good cup helps them stay.

Gyms and wellness studios. A post-class cold brew or matcha that feels like a reward.

Coworking spaces. A real café corner without building one.

Boutiques and makers' shops. Something warm to hold while people browse.

Masjids and community centers. After jumu'ah, after class, after the meeting runs long.

Something else? If your space has people and a little room, let's talk.
